Blackjack Card Counting & Basic Strategy Tool

Configurable Advantage Play Detection, Hi-Lo Tracking & Player Compliance System

Hi-Lo Logic

Filters: Date, Player ID, Table Name, Shoe ID.

Hi-Lo Values: Low (2–6) = +1 | High (10, J, Q, K, A) = -1 | Middle (7, 8, 9) = 0.

True Count Formula: Count Score divided by remaining decks.

Basic Strategy Compliance Tiers

Evaluates move accuracy across Hard, Soft, and Pairs:

90–100%: Mostly followed
70–90%: Mainly followed
40–70%: Rarely followed
0–40%: Doesn't follow

Configurable Alert Criteria

Triggers notifications and sets "Playing regarding True Count" = YES in Winner Report:

1
Wager ≥ 3× player's prior average bet (e.g. $100 bet vs $25 avg).
2
High-True-Count avg bet exceeds table's other bets avg by ≥ 3×.
3
Wagers placed at TC > +2 account for ≥ 60% of total bet amount.

Winner Report Structure & Logics

Exportable PDF Single-Player Audit Tool & Integrated Analytical Frameworks

Report Sections (1 to 8)

1. General Information: Username, External ID, Brand, First/Last Bet dates, Lifetime GGR/Turnover, Lifetime Margin %, and Risk Assessment.
2. Investigation Data: UTC Start/End window metrics, session GGR, Turnover, Margin %, and Total Games played.
3. General Financial Data per Game Type: Turnover, GGR, and Margin across all categories played during window.
4. Detailed Financial Data per Table: Granular round count, turnover, and margin broken down by table names.
5. Gameplay Financial Analysis: Wagering patterns, min/max/avg round bets, winning/losing round counts, outcome %.
6. Strategy Analysis (Game-Specific): Custom behavioral breakdowns per game category.
7. Game Pattern & Conclusion: Qualitative text fields for manual analysis and final Risk Officer verdicts.
8. Financial Data per Days: Daily chronological performance log displaying turnover, GGR, and margin.

Integrated Analytical Logics

• Blackjack Strategy & Card Counting: Hi-Lo True Count tracking (YES/NO) & Basic Strategy accuracy (90-100%, 70-90%, 40-70%, 0-40%).
• Bet Continuity & Session Breaks: Consecutive bet ratio (≥50% vs <50%) and skipped round breaks (Short: 5-10, Long: 11-20, None: 0-5 or >20).
• Shoe Coverage Analysis: Start of New Shoe (≥1 bet in first 33%) and All Over Shoe (≥1 bet in each 1/3 of shoe).
• Betting Progression: Classifies Positive Progression, Negative Progression, Flat Wagers, or Chaotic sizing.
• Roulette Bet Patterning: 100% matching spot combos across tables & Wheel Coverage (Sequential Rim, Layout, Random %).
• Statistical Z-Score Deviations: Standard deviation vs theoretical model: σ_theoretical = √( Σ (k_i² · b_i²) ) Categorized as Normal (≤ ±2), Rare (±3 to ±4), or Borderline Impossible (±5+).
